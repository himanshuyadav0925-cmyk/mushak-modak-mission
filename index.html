🐭 Mushak: Modak Mission 🐭

Ganpati Bappa Morya!

Help Mushak, Lord Ganesha's loyal companion, collect as many delicious modaks as possible! Avoid obstacles, collect bonuses, and complete the level before time runs out.

A festive game for Ganesh Chaturthi 2024

How to Play

🎮 Controls

Desktop: Arrow Keys or WASD to move

Mobile: Use on-screen arrow buttons or swipe

🎯 Objective

  • Collect 50 modaks to complete the level
  • Avoid obstacles that block your path
  • Grab bonus items for extra points
  • Complete the level before time runs out

✨ Scoring

  • 🍪 Normal Modak = +10 points
  • 💎 Bonus Item = +25 points
  • ❤️ Lose a life when hit by obstacle

🎪 Game Features

  • 3 lives to complete the level
  • Difficulty increases as you progress
  • Celebration when you win!
